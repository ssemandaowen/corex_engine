### Check regarding `BACKTEST_UPLOAD_CREATED/DELETED/ARCHIVED`
`BACKTEST_UPLOAD_CREATED`, `BACKTEST_UPLOAD_DELETED`, and `BACKTEST_UPLOAD_ARCHIVED` do **NOT** exist in `WS_EVENT_TYPES` in `config/constants.js`. They are listed in `WS_USER_SCOPED_EVENT_TYPES` set in `engine/services/broadcaster.js:100-102` as legacy string literals for potential upload events, but are not constants in `config/constants.js`.
